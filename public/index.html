Rulebook & Guidelines

Important rules and registration process

General Rules

+
  • All participants must be VGEC students with valid ID cards.
  • Registration is mandatory for all events before the deadline.
  • Follow event timings strictly. Late arrivals may be disqualified.
  • Maintain discipline and respect event organizers and volunteers.
  • No unfair means, cheating, or misconduct will be tolerated.
  • Decisions of judges and organizers are final and binding.
  • Participants are responsible for their personal belongings.

Event-Specific Rules

+

HUNT-A-THON

  • Teams of exactly 4 members required.
  • Registration fee: ₹100 per team.
  • Time limit will be announced at the start.
  • No use of external help or digital tools for clues.

GAME GALA & THINK BITS

  • Individual or team participation based on game.
  • Each game has specific time limits.
  • No retries unless specified by organizers.

Cultural Evening

  • Valid college ID mandatory for entry.
  • Wear comfortable clothes.
  • Maintain decorum during garba and jamming.
